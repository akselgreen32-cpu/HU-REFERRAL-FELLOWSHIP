About Us

Who we are

Our fellowship was founded in 1997 E.C. by a small group of Christian students on campus. It began with three teams and today serves through seven. We are part of the Evangelical Students and Graduates Union of Ethiopia (EvaSUE).

Our aim is to help students grow in personal spiritual life, worship God together, and share Christ with the friends around them, and to equip every member to take an active part in the church after graduation.

Our Teams

Seven teams, one shared calling

New members choose a team after their first three months in the fellowship.

01

Action Team

Shows love in practice through hospital ward visits, street children, needy families, and prison visits.

02

Berea Team

Bible-centered ministry: follow-up and basic teaching for new believers, Bible study coordination, and Daily Bread.

03

Evangelism Mobilizing Team

Leads and mobilizes evangelism on and off campus through personal sharing, Prayer-Care-Share, and outreach.

04

Kebron Choir Team

Leads worship through song and develops members in music, songwriting, and instruments.

05

Levites Literature & Drama

Builds up the fellowship through written pieces, literature nights, and drama.

06

Prayer Mobilizing Team

Mobilizes the whole fellowship to pray: daily devotion, lunch prayer, a prayer week, and fasting days.

07

Welfare Team

Counseling and care: follows up on members who drift away, offers counseling (including via a Telegram bot), and lends spiritual books.

Standing Committees

Digitals · Fundraising · Mission

Digitals handles graphics, video, photo, and sound. The Fund committee raises resources, and the Mission committee organizes the annual mission trip.

Yearly Events

Moments that shape our year

I

Teams Week

Two days of games, worship, and fellowship for members.

II

Fresh Welcome

The day we welcome new Christian students and bring them into the fellowship family.

III

Conference

At least once a year, over two to three days, with guest speakers and worship leaders.

IV

Batch Day & Graduate Send-off

Strengthening each batch, and sending graduating students off with prayer and certificates.

V

Leaders Big Retreat

Three days of prayer, review, and planning, where the year's Big Picture theme is introduced.

Mission Service

Taking the gospel to the unreached

Since 2013 E.C. we have sent missionaries to the Tsemay and Erbore areas of South Omo, where the gospel has not widely reached, and we run a mission trip every summer.

  • Mission belongs to the whole fellowship and continues as leaders change.
  • We commit to each area for at least three years.
What We Believe

The EvaSUE statement of faith

Our members come from many evangelical churches. Together we hold firmly to the core of the Christian faith.

  • One God: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit.
  • The Bible is God-inspired and our final authority in faith and life.
  • Salvation comes through the sacrificial death and resurrection of Jesus Christ.
  • Sinners are justified by grace alone through faith.
  • The Holy Spirit lives and works in every believer.
  • One church, the body of Christ, and his visible return.
