Our History

Founded on the grounds of Hawassa University's medical campus, our fellowship began as a small gathering of health science students seeking to balance rigorous academics with spiritual growth.

Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant community, providing a foundation of support, spiritual guidance, and ethical grounding for future medical professionals.

About Us

The HU Referral Fellowship is a Christian organization dedicated to supporting students through their medical and academic journeys.

We believe in holistic development—nurturing the mind through science and the spirit through faith. We aim to equip healthcare leaders who will serve with compassion and excellence.

Mission Service

As future healthcare professionals, our mission goes beyond the campus. We conduct regular outreach programs in the surrounding areas.

From organizing medical camps and health education seminars to visiting patients in the referral hospital, we integrate our medical knowledge with our calling to serve the vulnerable.
